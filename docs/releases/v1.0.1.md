**A faster nuza.** Opening, switching, creating, renaming and deleting notes now take a frame or two, in vaults of any size.

## Faster

- **Switching notes** is up to four times faster. Each switch used to check every link in the vault against every note, and to restyle the whole window.
- **Creating, renaming and opening folders** no longer wait behind nuza's background scan of the vault. A rename could take three seconds in a large vault; it now takes a fraction of a second.
- **Deleting** is instant, the first time in a session too.
- **The AI panel** no longer slows typing or switching when it holds a long chat.

## Fixed

- A tab could be left faded, with a bar beside it, after a drag.
- New files and folders go where you'd expect: into the folder selected in the sidebar, or beside the selected note, as in VS Code. A folder you have just made is where the next note goes.
- The chat list in the AI panel is clearer: grouped by day, with the note each chat was about, a search once there are many, and a second press to delete.
